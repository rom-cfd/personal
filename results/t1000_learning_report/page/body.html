Online Learning Study

When the five-member committee disagrees (R > 0.3 before ignition), Cantera labels 1 000 new pairs from 100 perturbed starts × 100 steps and the members are fine-tuned. The question: should that knowledge be used online, per trajectory, or be consolidated into one offline model? The last experiment consolidates it with strategy 5's own 10-step physics-in-loop objective plus replay of the original data. Everything is compared against strategy 5 and the gated hybrid on the same 36 cases.

Iterated rounds

Each round: run the gym with the previous round's offline model (all 72 train + val cases, 2 passes; a data round when R > 0.3 before ignition, plus one forced round at the first 50 K rise in every case that did not trigger), turn every new pair into an 11-step Cantera window, pool the windows of all rounds so far, add as many original UR10 windows (1:1), and continue UR10 physics-in-loop training from the previous round's members. Test cases are never visited. The loop ends when a round's gym finds no point with R > 0.3.

UR10 consolidation with replay: training

Each of the 138 000 gym pairs was re-integrated by Cantera for 11 steps, giving 10-step windows (step 1 matches the saved label to 2×10⁻⁶ K). These were mixed with randomly drawn windows from strategy 5's own 4.9 M UR10 training set at 1:1 or 1:3. Training continues strategy 5's recipe from its best-validation weights: 10-step unrolled MAE with Wang projection and enthalpy T inside the loop, pressure pinned, Adam 1e-5 (×0.4 at epoch 15), 30 epochs, best weights selected on the unrolled validation loss. Interpretation and next steps

  • Per-trajectory online fine-tuning works. It fixes all five slow cases, reaching 100% correct ignition with a median τ error matching strategy 5, without the ODE solver ever advancing the solution. It is still behind the gated hybrid on the longest inductions (#7, #9), and costs 11× more ODE steps (as training labels) plus about 40 CPU-minutes of fine-tuning.
  • One offline model does not absorb the knowledge well. Sequential gym updates forgot: the regular cases degraded about 15×, and pass 2 needed nearly as many rounds as pass 1. Consolidating all gym data at once is much better: lower mean T RMSE than strategy 5 and the worst τ error cut from 172% to 29%. But it still loses accuracy in the regular cases, and EWC only moves along that trade-off.
  • The objective mismatch was the main problem. Consolidating the same gym data with strategy 5's own 10-step physics-in-loop loss plus replay removes the forgetting: regular test cases become slightly better than strategy 5. Every summary measure on the held-out test improves.
  • What is still missing offline is the very long induction. Case 7 (5.3 ms) ignites in 2 of 5 seeds and #69 is at about 10% τ error. A 10-step window covers 20 µs, while case 7's induction is about 2 600 steps; small per-step biases that windows of this length cannot see still add up.
  • Possible next steps. (1) Longer unroll windows, or more gym passes with replay, concentrated on the 1000–1060 K induction region. (2) A coarser time step for the induction regime, so that each step's change is larger than the model's error. (3) A pragmatic combination: deploy the UR10-consolidated model offline and keep the disagreement gate as a safety net for the rare long-induction states.

Definitions

Trigger and round
R > 0.3 (20 µs rolling median) while some seed is below T₀ + 50 K. A round takes 100 starts perturbed around the seeds' states (T ± 5 K, mass fractions scaled by a log-normal factor with σ = 10%), runs Cantera for 100 steps of 2 µs each, keeps a random 1 000 pairs (900 to train, 100 to validate), and fine-tunes all 5 members.
Fine-tune (online, gym)
MAE on standardized increments (the pretraining loss, pressure pinned) + 10⁻³·Σ(θ−θ₀)² (L2-SP), Adam 1e-5, batch 100, up to 200 epochs, patience 30, keeping the best-validation weights. 1e-5 was chosen on validation case 52; 1e-4 did not learn.
Gym
72 train + val initial conditions, 2 shuffled passes. Weights carry across episodes, and the L2-SP anchor is the original weights. Snapshots are taken at the end of each pass.
EWC consolidation
One fine-tune from the strategy-5 weights on all 138 000 gym pairs (10% of rounds held out) with MAE + λ·Σ Fᵢ(θᵢ−θ₀ᵢ)². F is the diagonal empirical Fisher of the pretraining loss on 20 000 original training pairs, with λ = c / mean(F). Adam 1e-5, batch 256, up to 40 epochs. The epoch is selected on the mean of the relative gym-validation and original-validation MAE.
Selection
c is chosen by the lowest validation mean 20-ms T RMSE, with ignition classification as the tie-break; this rule was fixed before the test numbers were seen. Validation cases are in-gym; test cases were never visited by any learning step.
Offline
Each seed rolls out alone with Wang projection and enthalpy temperature, exactly as strategy 5: no committee, no updates, no ODE.
