Metric definitions

Completed
The rollout reached the horizon without a non-finite or undefined Box–Cox inverse output.
Physically valid
Completed, and at every step: 0 ≤ Yₖ ≤ 1, |ΣY − 1| ≤ 1 %, element mass fractions within 0.01 of the initial state, 200 K ≤ T ≤ 6000 K, and pressure within 1 % of its initial value.
Ignition classification
The rollout and the reference agree on whether T rises 400 K above T₀ within the horizon.
τ₄₀₀ error
|τ₄₀₀,pred − τ₄₀₀,ref| / τ₄₀₀,ref, where τ₄₀₀ is the interpolated time of a 400 K rise. The reference uses a 0.2 µs fine-step solution. Only rollouts where both ignite are counted.
T RMSE
Temperature RMSE over the full horizon against the reference. Means average over completed rollouts only, so raw strategies are favoured by dropping their crashed runs.
One-step T RMSE
Single 2 µs step from reference states on the test trajectories, no feedback.
Models: best-validation checkpoints (baseline by one-step validation MAE; UR10 by unrolled validation loss). The test set was not used for training, model selection or learning-rate decisions.
